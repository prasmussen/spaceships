Your first flight

Start in Flight lab’s open arena to learn momentum and land on the two pads, then try the Landing course. Open Controls to change keys, enable sound or reduce camera motion.

  1. Thrust in short bursts. Your ship gradually slows when you release thrust. Turn against your motion and thrust to brake faster.
  2. Release to steady your aim. Hold left or right to turn; release to quickly stop spinning.
  3. Land gently. Approach your own illuminated pad upright, with very little sideways speed or spin. Landing refuels your ship; other terrain contact crashes it.
  4. Win the duel. Hold fire to shoot. Three hits destroy a ship and award one point. Colliding with another ship destroys both ships. A crash costs you one point. First to five wins; tied winning scores continue until one player leads.

After a death you respawn in two seconds. Spawn protection lasts briefly and ends when you fire or leave the pad.

Play online

Each player opens the game in a separate browser. Open Online duel and connect. Create an invite and share its code, then both select Ready. Find opponent starts a match automatically when another player joins your region’s queue. Use Leave online to return to solo practice. After a finished match, both players can choose a rematch.

Connection stalls pause prediction while inputs catch up. Casual results are unverified. Capture replay saves confirmed play for the Rollback lab.
